# Rider Requirements FAQ

## How do I become a rider? What does a rider need to start?

To start as a rider, you need to come with:

- 4 passport photographs.
- Your Ghana Card.
- A Guarantor.

Your Guarantor must come with their own Ghana Card. The Guarantor must also read and understand the terms of the contract before it is signed.

## What documents do I need?

The rider needs 4 passport photographs and a Ghana Card. The Guarantor needs their own Ghana Card. Both the rider and the Guarantor must provide valid ID when signing, and their ID details are recorded on the agreement.

## What is a Guarantor and who can be one?

A Guarantor is a family member the rider brings along. If the rider stops paying and cannot be found, the Guarantor becomes responsible for the debt. The Guarantor must come with their own Ghana Card, and must read and understand the terms of the contract before signing.
