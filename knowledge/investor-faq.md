# RideIQ Investor FAQ

This document is for people who want to invest in RideIQ motorcycles as investors. It is separate from the rider documents. Riders are the people who ride and pay for a bike under the Work & Pay agreement. Investors are the people who fund the bikes.

## What is the RideIQ Investment Program?

RideIQ allows investors to fund the purchase and registration of commercial motorcycles while RideIQ manages the operational side of the investment.

As an investor, you provide the capital required to purchase and register the motorcycle. RideIQ manages the motorcycle, the rider relationship, payment monitoring and operational administration.

The standard investment amount is GHS 12,500 per bike, which covers the purchase and registration of the motorcycle.

The investment lasts 12 months, with a target return of 20% per year, subject to the applicable investment terms. The return is a target and is not a guarantee.

For more questions, contact RideIQ on WhatsApp: 0266181581.

## How much do I need to invest?

The standard capital needed is GHS 12,500 per bike. This includes the purchase and registration of the motorcycle.

Your total investment grows with the number of bikes you fund:

- 1 bike = GHS 12,500
- 2 bikes = GHS 25,000
- 3 bikes = GHS 37,500
- 5 bikes = GHS 62,500

## What return can I expect?

The current RideIQ investment structure targets a 20% return per year over a 12-month investment period. This is a target return, not a guaranteed return.

For one bike:

- Capital: GHS 12,500
- Target return of 20%: GHS 2,500
- Total scheduled payout: GHS 15,000

Your investment agreement sets out how and when your capital and returns are paid.

## How much will I receive if I invest more than one bike?

The capital, target return and total scheduled payout grow with the number of bikes:

- 1 bike: capital GHS 12,500, target return GHS 2,500, total GHS 15,000
- 2 bikes: capital GHS 25,000, target return GHS 5,000, total GHS 30,000
- 3 bikes: capital GHS 37,500, target return GHS 7,500, total GHS 45,000
- 5 bikes: capital GHS 62,500, target return GHS 12,500, total GHS 75,000

## How are investors paid?

Investors receive scheduled payments during the investment period. The exact weekly payment amount and payment schedule are set in each investor's investment agreement, so RideIQ does not quote a fixed weekly figure here. The scheduled payments grow with the number of bikes you fund.

For the weekly amount and schedule, contact RideIQ on WhatsApp: 0266181581.

## Can I invest in more than one bike?

Yes. An investor can fund several bikes, depending on the investment opportunities RideIQ has available and the applicable terms.

The number of bikes you fund decides your total capital and your scheduled payments.

## How long does the investment last?

The standard investment lasts 12 months. Your investment agreement states the start date, the payment schedule, the maturity date and the other applicable terms. The maturity date is 12 months after the investment starts.

## What happens if a rider misses a payment? (investor)

RideIQ monitors rider payments every week. Riders must follow their agreed payment schedule. A late payment counts as a strike against the rider. After the rider's grace strikes are used up, RideIQ can take action, including repossessing the motorcycle, as set out in the rider's agreement and the law.

## How does the grace period work? (investor)

The grace period is the rider's allowance for late payments. A payment is late if it is not made on the exact day it is due (for example, due on Monday and paid on Tuesday). Each late payment counts as one strike, even if the rider pays it a day later, and the strike is never removed. On the third late payment the motorcycle is repossessed.

## What happens to my investment if a rider misses payments?

RideIQ monitors rider payments and takes steps to protect the investment.

If a rider falls behind, RideIQ can start the recovery process. This can include contacting the rider, enforcing the payment plan, or repossessing the motorcycle where the agreement allows.

Any adjustment to investor payments caused by missed rider payments is handled according to the investment agreement.

## What happens if a rider defaults or misses several payments?

If a rider misses several scheduled payments, RideIQ follows its recovery and enforcement process. Where the investment terms provide for an adjustment or balancing payment, the investor's account can be reconciled according to the agreed schedule.

The exact treatment of missed payments is stated in the investor agreement.

## Do I have to manage the rider?

No. The RideIQ investment model lets the investor provide the capital while RideIQ manages the operations. RideIQ can handle:

- Rider assignment
- Payment monitoring
- Payment records
- Rider communication
- Motorcycle monitoring
- Maintenance coordination
- Payment reminders
- Default management
- Repossession processes where they apply

## Do I own the motorcycle? (investor)

Ownership depends on your investment agreement and the legal structure RideIQ uses. Read the investment agreement carefully to understand ownership, beneficial interests, security arrangements, and what happens to the motorcycle at the end of the investment period.

## Who pays for maintenance? (investor)

Under the rider's Work & Pay agreement, the rider pays for all maintenance and repairs at his own cost (tires, accident damage, engine oil, lights and brakes). RideIQ can coordinate maintenance. How repairs, accidents or damage affect an investor is set out in the investment agreement.

## What happens if the bike is damaged or stolen?

Accidents, damage and theft are risks of the investment. The investment agreement explains how they are managed and how they can affect an investor. For questions about a specific case, contact RideIQ on WhatsApp: 0266181581.

## Can I track the motorcycle?

Where GPS tracking is installed, RideIQ can monitor the motorcycle's location through the RideIQ management platform. Investors can be given suitable investment-level information, depending on RideIQ's policies.

- Total amount paid

## What happens when my 12-month investment ends?

At the end of the agreed 12-month period, RideIQ reconciles the investor's account according to the investment agreement. The investor should receive the outstanding capital and return amounts according to the agreed payment schedule and terms.

## Can I reinvest after my investment ends?

Yes, depending on the investment opportunities RideIQ has available. An investor can choose to reinvest their capital into more motorcycles or another available RideIQ investment opportunity.

## Can I increase my investment later?

Yes, depending on availability. An existing investor can fund more motorcycles. For example, an investor who starts with one bike can later add two more, which raises their total investment and scheduled payments.

## Is there a minimum investment?

The standard minimum investment is GHS 12,500, which is one motorcycle and its registration costs. More investment can be made in multiples of the per-bike amount of GHS 12,500.

## What information or documents do I need to become an investor?

Investors should expect to give the identification and information needed for onboarding and compliance. This can include:

- Full name

More information may be needed depending on the onboarding and compliance requirements.

## Can I withdraw my investment before 12 months?

Any early withdrawal or exit is handled according to the investment agreement. Review the early-exit terms before you commit capital.

## What risks should I consider?

Like any business or investment, the RideIQ investment model has risks. These can include:

- Rider payment defaults
- Motorcycle downtime
- Maintenance and repair costs
- Accidents or damage
- Theft
- Operational interruptions
- Recovery or repossession delays
- Changes in operating costs

The return is a target and is not guaranteed. The investment agreement explains how these risks are managed and how they can affect an investor.

## How does RideIQ protect the investment?

RideIQ uses operational controls such as:

- Rider screening
- Written rider agreements
- Weekly payment monitoring
- Payment reminders
- Grace-period monitoring
- GPS motorcycle tracking
- Maintenance monitoring
- Rider performance tracking
- Default alerts
- Repossession procedures where they apply

These controls help RideIQ monitor the motorcycles and manage rider obligations.

## How do I get started as an investor?

To become a RideIQ investor:

1. Choose the number of motorcycles you want to fund.
2. Review the investment terms.
3. Complete investor onboarding.
4. Give the required identification and information.
5. Sign the investment agreement.
6. Make your investment payment.
7. RideIQ buys and registers the motorcycle or motorcycles.
8. Your investment is added to your investor dashboard.
9. Scheduled payments begin according to the agreed investment schedule.
